# Contributing

Additions are welcome. The bar is low but it is real.

## Before you open a PR

- **Open the thing.** If it is code, clone it. If it claims to run, run it. Entries
  that nobody has checked are how a list stops being worth reading.
- **Write the description yourself.** Do not paste the repository's own tagline.
  Say what it does and why someone would spend an evening on it. One or two lines.
- **No dead projects without a reason.** Unmaintained is fine when the thing is
  still the clearest example of its idea. Say so in the line.
- **Put it in the right section**, and keep the section alphabetical-ish by
  relevance rather than strictly by name. Most useful first.

## Format

```
- [name](url) - What it does, and the thing that makes it worth opening. Language.
```

Sentence case, a full stop at the end, no marketing adjectives.

## What gets rejected

- Course projects and tutorials with no working output.
- Repositories that are a single notebook reproducing someone else's paper, unless
  the reproduction is meaningfully clearer than the original.
- Anything paywalled, closed-source, or requiring a signup to see it move.
- Self-promotion is fine. Self-promotion of something you have not finished is not.
